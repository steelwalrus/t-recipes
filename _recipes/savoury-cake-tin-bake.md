## Ingredients

- 1 carrot
- A handful of frozen peas
- 4 to 6 eggs
- A generous handful of grated cheddar
- 2 tbsp flour (optional)
- Oil or butter, for the tin

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Grease the cake tin well with oil or butter. If you have baking paper, line the base so it turns out easily.
2. If the tin has a loose base or a clip on the side, line it with one big sheet of baking paper pressed up the sides, and stand it on a baking tray. Raw egg leaks through the seam otherwise.
3. Grate the carrot. Diced carrot can stay hard, which is a choking risk. Put it in a microwave-safe bowl with the peas and 2 tbsp water, cover, and microwave for 2 minutes. Drain well.
4. Whisk the eggs in a large bowl. If you're using the flour, whisk it in now until smooth. It makes the bake fluffier and helps it hold together.
5. Stir in the cheddar, then the carrot and peas.
6. Pour into the tin and bake for 20 to 25 minutes. A deeper bake takes longer. It's done when a knife pushed into the middle comes out with no runny egg.
7. Leave it in the tin for a few minutes, then turn it out onto a board. Cut into strips or small squares.

## Serving

Good warm. Just as good cold from the fridge as a snack later. Put leftovers in the fridge within two hours and eat them within two days.

## Notes

- Changed from the Gemini version before the first try. The carrot is grated instead of diced, the flour goes in before the cheese, and a knife test replaces the wobble test.
- If it comes out rubbery, add a splash of milk to the eggs next time.
