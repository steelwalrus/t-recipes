## Ingredients

- 1 carrot
- A handful of frozen peas
- 4 to 6 eggs
- A generous handful of grated cheddar
- 2 tbsp flour (optional)
- Oil or butter, for the tin

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Grease the cake tin well with oil or butter. If you have baking paper, line the base so it turns out easily.
2. Dice the carrot small so it cooks through. Put it in a microwave-safe bowl with the peas and 2 tbsp water, cover, and microwave for 2 minutes. Drain.
3. Whisk the eggs in a large bowl.
4. Stir in the cheddar. For a fluffier bake that holds together better, whisk in the flour until smooth.
5. Stir in the carrot and peas.
6. Pour into the tin and bake for 20 to 25 minutes. It's done when the top is golden and the middle doesn't wobble when you gently shake the tin.
7. Leave it in the tin for a few minutes, then turn it out onto a board. Cut into strips or small squares.

## Serving

Good warm. Just as good cold from the fridge as a snack later.
