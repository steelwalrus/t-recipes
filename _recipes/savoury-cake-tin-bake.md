## Ingredients

- 1 carrot
- A handful of frozen peas
- 4 to 6 eggs
- A big handful of grated cheddar
- 2 tbsp flour (optional)
- Oil or butter, for the tin

## Method

1. Oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Grease the tin and line the base with baking paper. If it has a loose base, line the sides too and stand it on a tray.
2. Grate the carrot. Microwave with the peas and 2 tbsp water, covered, for 2 minutes. Drain.
3. Whisk the eggs, and the flour if using, until smooth.
4. Stir in the cheese, carrot and peas.
5. Pour into the tin. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until a knife in the middle comes out clean.
6. Cool for a few minutes, turn out and cut into fingers.

## Serving

Warm, or cold from the fridge. Keeps for 2 days.
